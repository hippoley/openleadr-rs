## Strategic moat, external reliance, and kill rules

Aim to create a **vendor-neutral, reproducible OpenADR VEN/VTN interoperability evidence format** that can be consumed by test runners and CI, not a redundant Rust SDK. The durable asset is versioned scenarios, negative oracles, trace provenance, multi-vendor receipts and independently reproducible failure diagnosis. Alternative models will automate boilerplate generation; they do not replace access to external devices, permissioned test endpoints, independent witnesses, or publicly verifiable interoperability results.

**Adoption threshold:** prioritize substantive external PR reviews, issues opened by independent vendors, downstream integrations, and CI runs in repos not maintained by the author. Stars and assistant-generated activity are not substitutes.

**Pivot trigger:** after 6–8 weeks with no independent implementation, independent re-run, or third-party issue/PR despite direct outreach and usable documentation, reduce investment. Reassess whether cross-protocol evidence interchange (OpenADR / OCPP / Matter) or upstream conformance tooling is a stronger opportunity. Do not claim standards authority.

**Standard boundary:** Alliance certification uses its official test assets/tool/licensing process. Public engineering benchmark receipts are *not* certification and cannot substitute for it. Reference: https://www.openadr.org/openadr-3-certification
